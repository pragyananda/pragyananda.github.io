AURA streamlines taxation and financial data processing for ride-share drivers in Australia. Extraction modules ingest UBER and DIDI activity statements alongside automated pipelines for major Australian banks (CBA, NAB, ANZ). Phase 1 delivered a scalable architecture with a Business Activity Statement (BAS) automation engine for tax compliance, transaction reconciliation algorithms, and a dynamic user interface with Excel-integrated reporting for Australian financial-year requirements.

**Stack:** Python, data pipelines, reconciliation, Excel reporting.
