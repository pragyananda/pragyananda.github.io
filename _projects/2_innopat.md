Innopat automates patent drafting. Given the claims and an Invention Disclosure Form (IDF), it generates a complete patent submission: background, summary, detailed description, and claims, using RAG over prior art together with LLMs. I contributed to the generation pipeline and its evaluation at CAIR Lab.

**Stack:** Python, LangChain, RAG, large language models.
